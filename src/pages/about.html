OUR STORY

From the concrete jungle to your wardrobe.

BORN FROM THE STREETS

In 2018, STREETWEAR CO. started in a basement studio in Brooklyn. No investors. No business plan. Just a love for hip-hop culture, graffiti art, and the raw energy of the streets.

We were tired of seeing mass-produced fashion that had nothing to do with real street culture. Every brand was copying the aesthetic without understanding the culture.

So we decided to create something different. Premium quality. Limited quantities. Authentic designs inspired by the graffiti walls, late-night cyphers, and the electric energy of city nights.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

🎨

AUTHENTIC CULTURE

We don't just use graffiti as a design trend. We collaborate with real street artists, support local hip-hop venues, and keep the culture alive through everything we create.

💎

PREMIUM QUALITY

We use 6.5oz heavyweight pre-shrunk cotton, premium screen printing, and reinforced stitching. Every piece is built to survive the streets and look good doing it.

🔥

LIMITED DROPS

We'll never mass-produce. Each design is limited to 50-200 pieces worldwide. When they sell out, they're gone forever. No restocks. No compromises.

🌍

COMMUNITY FIRST

10% of every sale goes back to supporting local street artists, youth hip-hop programs, and community art initiatives. Real culture, real impact.
